Privacy Policy for Leaf Sweep

Last updated: 10 September 2026

This privacy policy explains how Leaf Sweep ("the app") handles information. Leaf Sweep is developed by an independent developer based in the United Kingdom.

The short version

Leaf Sweep does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to us or to any third party. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no data leaves your device except for purchase processing, which is handled entirely by Apple.

What information exists, and where it lives

Gameplay stats. Leaf Sweep keeps track of things like session history and personal bests, purely to show them back to you in the Stats screen. This information is stored only on your device. It is never sent anywhere, and it is permanently deleted the moment you delete the app.

Purchase records. When you unlock all styles via the in-app purchase, that transaction is processed and recorded entirely by Apple's In-App Purchase system (StoreKit). We don't receive, see, or store your payment details, name, or any other personal information connected to the purchase — Apple handles this according to its own privacy practices, which you can review at https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

What Leaf Sweep does not do

  • It does not create an account or profile for you.
  • It does not collect your name, email address, location, or any other personal identifier.
  • It does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking software of any kind.
  • It does not connect to the internet to send or receive information about you.
  • It does not share, sell, or otherwise disclose any information, because none is collected.

Children's privacy

Leaf Sweep does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the app collects no personal data at all, this applies equally regardless of the user's age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future update introduces a feature that does collect information — this page will be updated, and the "Last updated" date above will reflect that change. Any such change would also be reflected in the app's data disclosures on the App Store.
