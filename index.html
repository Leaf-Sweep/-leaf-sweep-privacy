Leaf Sweep Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

The short version: Leaf Sweep does not collect your data. Everything the game remembers stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Leaf Sweep does not collect, store or send any personal information to the developer or to any third party. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress and settings on your device only, for example: your garden, your leaf amount setting, your days in a row, total days cleared and badges, whether you have supported the game, and your sound and display choices. This information is used only to run the game and the Stats screen. It is never sent to us.

The game uses your device’s clock and time zone to work out how many leaves have gathered since your last visit and which day you cleared the garden. This happens on your device and nothing about it is sent anywhere.

Backups and deleting your data

Because this information is saved on your device, it is included in your iPhone’s normal iCloud or computer backup if you use one. This is handled by Apple, not by us. Deleting the app removes the game’s saved progress from your device. Copies held in your own backups are not affected until they are replaced or deleted. If you used an earlier version of Leaf Sweep, a small marker saved in your device’s Keychain may also remain after you delete the app. It holds no personal information. We never have a copy.

Purchases

Leaf Sweep is free to play. An optional one-time purchase, “Support Leaf Sweep”, adds the Straw Broom and Night Mode. It is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. When the game starts, it asks the App Store for the price in your local currency, and if you tap Restore Purchase, the game checks your purchase with Apple. We never see or store your payment details, your Apple ID or your name.

Links

The Support screen has links that open your Mail app (to contact us) and Safari (to open this policy). Those apps handle anything you send or view there under their own policies.

Children

Leaf Sweep does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. It contains no ads, no chat and no links to other players.

Changes

If a future version ever changes what Leaf Sweep collects, this page and the App Store privacy details will be updated before it is released.
